Common questions

Cannot find your answer? Send us a message and we will reply promptly.

How do I request a quotation?

Use the enquiry form below with your product list, quantities and delivery country. We will respond with pricing and lead times.

Do you supply products across India and internationally?

Yes. We cater to domestic orders across India as well as international trade and export shipments. Tell us your location or destination port and we will confirm delivery timelines.

Can I get technical data sheets before ordering?

Yes. Technical data sheets (PDS) and safety data sheets (MSDS) are available on request for all Sika products.

Which documents come with a shipment?

We provide all necessary transit paperwork including tax invoices, e-way bills for domestic transit, or commercial invoices, packing lists, and certificates required for exports.

Do you handle large project orders?

Yes. We plan bulk and phased deliveries around your site schedule. Share your project timeline with the enquiry.
